Trophy sounds by Spade (free).

sounds\trophy_bronze.mp3, trophy_silver.mp3, trophy_gold.mp3 and trophy_platinum.mp3 are the
default achievement sounds of Dragon Ball Z: Burst Limit Recompiled (achievement_sound = "tiers":
one sound per trophy tier - bronze, silver, gold - and the platinum one when every achievement is
unlocked). They were made by Spade, who shares them for free, and are included with this credit.
Changes: levels raised, converted to 48 kHz MP3.

To use other sounds, point achievement_sound_bronze / _silver / _gold / _platinum in
burstlimit.toml at your own .wav or .mp3 files. An empty or missing file falls back to a chime made
by the recomp.
